# 4TheAmazingWoman - Fast Landing Page

Clean, minimalist, and responsive mobile-first landing page for **4THEAMAZINGWOMAN Resources & Investment Ltd**.

## Brand Identity Applied
- **Primary Color:** Deep Emerald Green (`#061A13`, `#0B2B20`)
- **Background:** Sleek Black (`#080A09`)
- **Accents:** Metallic Gold (`#D4AF37`)
- **Typography:** Warm Cream (`#FDFBF7`) with Cormorant Garamond & Plus Jakarta Sans
- **Icons:** Pure SVG (no text emojis per brand guidelines)

## Features Included
1. **Hero Section:** Official CAC Registration (RC 7416346), brand headline, community stats.
2. **Core Pillars:** Private counseling, 4Brides2be Pre-wedding class, and Women's Empowerment.
3. **Product Catalog & Pricing:**
   - Paid Coaching Group (₦2,000)
   - 4Brides2be Pre-wedding Class (₦10,000)
   - The Ideal Homemaker Class (₦5,000)
   - Advertise Your Business Class (₦1,000)
4. **Interactive WhatsApp Enrollment Modal:** Automatically pre-fills the official WhatsApp (+234 812 541 7903) with the selected course name, price, and student details.
5. **Private 1-on-1 Counseling Booking:** Intake form routing appointment requests with topic and preferred timeframe directly to WhatsApp.
6. **Community & Founder:** 3,000+ member Telegram CTA and Zainab Abdullahi Hassan bio.
7. **Coming Soon App Banner:** Teaser for the full mobile/web app.

## How to Preview & Deploy
- **Local Preview:** Simply double-click `index.html` to open it in any web browser.
- **Deploy Live (Free):** Drag and drop this folder onto [Netlify Drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop) or [Vercel](https://vercel.com) for an instant live URL.
